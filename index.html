01 · Brain

Microcontroller

The microcontroller makes all the decisions about how to control the servos (stops 06 & 07) and the throttle. It also has a built-in Wi-Fi chip, so there's little to no latency between Bajaj's computer and the drone.

02 · Power

On/off switch

How Zephyr turns on and off.

03 · Sensing

Internal Measurement Unit (IMU)

This is a tilt sensor that relays information on tilt back to the microcontroller so it can counteract severe tilt.

04 · Power

Voltage Monitor

This allows Bajaj to monitor his battery such that he doesn't draw his battery down to absolute zero charge.

05 · Signals

Servo wiring

This is the wiring that relays information from the microcontroller to the servos.

06 · Speed control

ESC wiring bundle

Zephyr has two motors, meaning there are two electronic speed controllers (ESCs). Since there's no simple way to combine the two ESCs' outputs into one, Bajaj has resorted to using converters as shown in the image.

07 · Steering

Top servo

The top servo controls pitch.

08 · Steering

Bottom servo

The bottom servo controls roll.

09 · Thrust

Motors and fans

These are the motors and fans whose direction is controlled by the top servo and bottom servo, to make instantaneous adjustments within the air.

10 · Structure

Landing legs and frame

This is the frame and the base of Zephyr. It is a pyramidal structure.
